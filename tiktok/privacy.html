한국어

English

  • Heeyong Routine is a personal tool used only by its operator with the operator's own TikTok account. It does not accept sign-ups or logins from other users.
  • It uses only the user.info.basic scope (to confirm the account name) and the video.publish scope (to post videos the operator has approved).
  • It posts only videos and captions that the operator filmed or wrote and approved one by one.
  • It does not collect other users' data, comments, messages, or follower information.
  • Information received is never shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, and the operator can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings.
